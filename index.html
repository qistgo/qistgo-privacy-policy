QistGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

QistGo is a personal offline expense and balance tracking app that allows users to monitor their personal balance, income, and daily expenses. This policy explains what data the app stores, how it is used, and how local synchronization works.

Data the App Stores & Local Storage

All data entered into the application is stored locally on the user's personal device. The app may store personal records (balance, income, expenses, categories, dates, and notes), app preferences, and local authentication passcodes.

Local Network Synchronization (LAN-Node)

QistGo features an optional local network (LAN) synchronization capability. If enabled by the user, the app connects directly with the user's own companion software running on a local computer within the same local network (Wi-Fi/LAN).

This synchronization shares and syncs database records between the user's personal phone and their personal computer.

No Internet Cloud: Data is transmitted exclusively over the local network and is never uploaded to external cloud servers or third-party internet services.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Access

QistGo does not sell, rent, or share user data with advertising networks, analytics providers, or external third parties. Data sharing is strictly restricted to the user's own local computer when LAN synchronization is manually activated by the user.
