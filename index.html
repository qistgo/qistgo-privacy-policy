About QistGo

Personal Expense & Record Organizer

Take full control of your daily personal records and budget with QistGo, a clean, private, and offline-first productivity tool designed to help you replace traditional paper notebooks. It is built to help you organize your personal notes, lists, and daily expenses effortlessly on your own device.

Whether you want to keep track of daily expenses, record personal reminders, or manage your personal database entries, QistGo provides a simple and secure environment right in the palm of your hand.

Key Features

  • 100% Offline & Private: All your data remains strictly on your device. No cloud servers, no forced online accounts, and no data tracking.
  • Personal Expense Tracking: Easily add, organize, and monitor your personal budget and daily expenses in one place, just like a digital notebook.
  • Local Records & Notes: Keep detailed records of your personal text entries, amounts, dates, and personal memos.
  • Backup & Restore: Easily create local backups of your database or import data whenever needed, giving you full control over your files.
  • Local Security (Passcode): Protect your private entries by enabling a secure local passcode to prevent unauthorized access on your device.
  • Clean & Simple Interface: Enjoy a user-friendly, lightweight design that makes personal organization fast and straightforward.

Data Stored Locally

QistGo may store records entered by the user, including wallet balances, transactions, personal contacts, amounts, notes, app settings, and a local passcode. This data is stored strictly on the user's device and is never transmitted to external servers or cloud services.

Backup and Import

If the user chooses to create a backup or import a database file, the selected file is handled only for that user-requested action. Backup files are saved to the location selected by the user (e.g., device storage, SD card, or external storage). The user maintains full control over backup files and their management.

Data Collection and Sharing

QistGo does not collect, sell, rent, or share user data with advertising networks, analytics providers, external servers, or third parties. The app does not require a user account and operates entirely offline on the user's device.

Security

QistGo provides a local passcode feature to help protect access to the app. Users are responsible for keeping their device, passcode, and backup files secure.

Disclaimer

QistGo is an independent personal productivity tool intended solely for individual personal use. It acts as a digital alternative to paper notebooks and does not provide banking, lending, financial advisory, or commercial enterprise services.

الميزات الرئيسية

  • بدون إنترنت وخصوصية تامة (100% Offline): تبقى جميع بياناتك مخزنة على جهازك حصرياً. لا توجد خوادم سحابية، ولا حسابات إلزامية عبر الإنترنت، ولا تتبع للبيانات.
  • تتبع المصروفات الشخصية: أضف ونظم وراقب ميزانيتك الشخصية ومصروفاتك اليومية في مكان واحد بسهولة، تماماً مثل الدفتر الرقمي.
  • سجلات وملاحظات محلية: احتفظ بسجلات مفصلة لمدخلاتك النصية الشخصية، والمبالغ، والتواريخ، والمذكرات الشخصية.
  • النسخ الاحتياطي والاستيراد: أنشئ نسخاً احتياطية محلية لقاعدة البيانات أو استورد بياناتك متى احتجت إلى ذلك، مما يمنحك تحكماً كاملاً بملفاتك.
  • حماية محلية (رمز دخول): احمِ مدخلاتك الخاصة عبر تفعيل رمز مرور محلي آمن لمنع الوصول غير المصرح به على جهازك.
  • واجهة نظيفة وبسيطة: استمتع بتصميم خفيف وسهل الاستخدام يجعل التنظيم الشخصي سريعاً ومباشراً.
